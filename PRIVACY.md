# Privacy Policy — Topic Blocker for 247Sports

Topic Blocker for 247Sports does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What is stored:** the usernames you add to your blocklist.
- **Where it is stored:** in Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome keeps in your browser and, if you have Chrome sync turned on, syncs through your own Google account to your other Chrome browsers.
- **Who can see it:** only you. The extension has no server, no analytics and no third-party code, and its developer never receives your blocklist or any information about your browsing.
- **What it reads:** on 247sports.com board pages, the extension reads the author name shown on each topic so it can remove topics by users on your blocklist. This happens entirely inside your browser.

Removing the extension deletes its stored data from your browser.

Topic Blocker for 247Sports is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by 247Sports or CBS.
